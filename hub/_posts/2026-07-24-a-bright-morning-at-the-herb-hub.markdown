The morning of July 24, 2026, brought a steady climb in activity within our indoor greenhouse. As the sun rose, light levels surged from a dim 20 lux at dawn to a vibrant 835.8 lux by mid-morning. This natural progression was accompanied by a comfortable rise in air temperature, which warmed from a cool 18.02°C to a pleasant 22.09°C by our latest reading.

Our herbs are currently thriving with remarkably consistent hydration. While the ambient humidity dipped from 60.5% to just under 50% as the environment warmed, soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano remained at a perfect 100%. These damp root zones provide a stable, reliable foundation for the day’s growth.

The vitality of the plants is easy to see in our latest snapshot. On the left, the basil displays lush, glossy green leaves that appear vibrant and full of life. To the right, the chilli plant is making great progress; a small, bright green pepper is clearly visible and developing well, supported by a sturdy wooden stake.

Individual soil temperatures followed the rising ambient trend, with the oregano and basil reaching highs of approximately 18.6°C.
