As of August 5th, the greenhouse is thriving under the midday sun. Sensors recorded a light peak of 1246.7 lux, providing ample energy for photosynthesis. Ambient temperatures have risen from a morning low of 22.1°C to a warm 25.1°C, creating a highly productive environment for our indoor garden.

Hydration remains optimal across the board, with soil moisture levels for the basil, chilli, and oregano holding steady at 100%. While humidity naturally dipped from 53.4% to 37.8% as the air warmed, the plants show no signs of stress. They continue to flourish, benefiting from the intensified natural light.

The basil is particularly striking today, displaying vibrant lime-green leaves and a lush, vigorous habit. Nearby, the chilli plant is making excellent progress; a small, firm green chilli is now clearly visible among the foliage, confirming that current conditions are perfectly supporting fruit development.

Overall, the greenhouse remains in a remarkably stable state. Internal plant temperatures are averaging a cool 21°C, suggesting the herbs are transpiring effectively even as the surrounding air warms. We will continue to monitor these steady conditions as we transition into the evening hours.
