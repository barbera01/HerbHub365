It was a peaceful start at [Herb Hub 365](https://www.herbhub365.com) on July 15, 2026. As the sun began to peek over the horizon, our sensors captured a gentle transition from the cool night into the early morning hours. The greenhouse environment remained steady and comfortable, with temperatures averaging a mild 18.99°C.

Light levels provided a soft wake-up call for the plants, climbing from a near-dark 0.8 lux at 6:00 AM to 599.2 lux by mid-morning. During this time, humidity rose slightly from 60% to a peak of 65.1%. This gradual increase in brightness allows the herbs to adjust naturally without the stress of immediate, intense heat.

The herbs are well-hydrated and ready for the day ahead. Soil moisture for the basil remained at a consistent 100%, while the oregano and chilli followed closely at 93.6% and 86.8% respectively. These high levels ensure the roots have plenty of resources as the growing medium remains thoroughly saturated.

Our early morning snapshot captures the greenhouse still draped in deep shadows, reflecting the low-light readings from the start of the session. The image shows the dark silhouettes of dense foliage and a central support stake against a soft glow from a doorway in the background. It is a moment of quiet stillness before the full activity of the day begins.
