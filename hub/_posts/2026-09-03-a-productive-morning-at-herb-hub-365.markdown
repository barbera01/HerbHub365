The greenhouse enjoyed a steady, bright start this September 3rd. Between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM, light levels climbed from a soft 56.7 lux to a vibrant 715 lux. With air temperatures averaging 23°C and humidity holding steady in the mid-50% range, the environment provided a stable foundation for the morning growth spurt.

Our sensors indicate that the basil, chilli, and oregano plants are exceptionally well-hydrated, with soil moisture levels reaching 100% following recent care. The plants themselves remained slightly cooler than the surrounding air at 21°C. This temperature difference is a positive sign of active metabolism and healthy transpiration across the herb garden.

Visual progress remains encouraging, particularly for the basil on the left, which displays deep green, vibrant leaves. In the background, a bright orange chilli pepper has reached a beautiful, mature hue. This splash of color stands out sharply against the foliage, signaling that harvest time is quickly approaching for our peppers.

While the basil thrives, we are keeping a close eye on the chilli plants in the foreground, where some leaves show a paler, yellowish tint and minor browning at the tips. We will continue to monitor these small spots and the overall greenhouse environment to ensure a healthy transition into the autumn season.
