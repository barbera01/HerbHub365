This vitality is backed by our sensors, which show soil moisture at a consistent 100% for the basil, chilli, and oregano alike.

With air pressure holding steady at 1015 hPa and internal temperatures following the room's gentle upward trend, the greenhouse remains a stable and productive space. We are monitoring moisture levels closely to ensure they stay consistent as the heat builds, but for now, the Herb Hub 365 family is looking healthy and full of vigor.
