The greenhouse saw a steady climb in activity during the early hours of July 16, 2026. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, the indoor temperature rose from a cool 19.68°C to a comfortable 22.87°C. This warming trend coincided with a significant increase in light levels, which peaked at 915.8 lux, signaling a bright start for the herbs.

The garden is looking vibrant and full of life. The basil is particularly lush, showing off dense, bright green foliage, while the staked chilli plant in the center is making great progress. It has produced a healthy, glossy green pepper that is now clearly visible. Although the chilli's larger leaves appear a bit paler than the deep green of the surrounding herbs, the plant remains upright and vigorous.

Soil moisture levels remain high, ensuring the plants are well-hydrated for the day ahead. Basil sensors reported a consistent 100% moisture level throughout the morning, while the chilli and oregano stayed stable at roughly 84%. Despite a very slight drying trend in the oregano as the light intensified, the environment remains ideal for growth.

As the temperature climbed, humidity naturally dipped from 57.1% down to 49.6%. Atmospheric pressure remained perfectly steady at 1021 hPa, providing a stable foundation for the morning's development. It has been a productive start to the day here at Herb Hub 365, and we look forward to seeing how the plants respond to the afternoon warmth.
