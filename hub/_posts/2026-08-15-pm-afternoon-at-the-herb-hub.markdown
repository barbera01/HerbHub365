The greenhouse warmed significantly today, reaching a peak of 31.28°C by early afternoon. As the sun moved overhead, light levels surged to over 4,700 lux, creating a bright and energetic environment for the herbs. This heat was accompanied by a sharp drop in humidity, which fell from a morning high of 53.7% to a much drier 28.7% by midday.

Our latest snapshot shows the basil thriving with broad, glossy, vibrant green leaves filling the frame. In the center, the chilli plant is making excellent progress; one prominent pepper has ripened to a warm orange, while a smaller green pepper develops higher up the stem, supported by its wooden stake.

While the plants look healthy, our sensors have flagged a concern for the chilli’s hydration. While the basil and oregano remain well-watered with moisture levels between 73% and 79%, the chilli soil has hit a critical low of 0%. The pot is drying out rapidly under the intense afternoon sun and requires immediate attention.

Despite the thirsty chilli, the greenhouse remains stable with air pressure holding steady at 1015 hPa. We are prioritizing hydration to ensure the ripening fruit and lush foliage continue to thrive through the rest of the day.
