It has been a steady, mild day in the greenhouse. Our sensors recorded temperatures climbing from 17.8°C this morning to a comfortable 20.76°C by midday. With humidity holding steady at around 53%, the environment remains stable and supportive for the entire collection as we move through the afternoon.

The highlight of the day is our chilli plant, which now boasts a single, bright green pepper hanging prominently from its branch. While the fruit looks strong, some of the larger leaves are showing a pale, yellowish-green hue. We are monitoring the soil moisture closely, as levels have fluctuated throughout the day and currently sit at 43.9%.

Nearby, the basil continues to thrive with impressive vigor. Its deep green leaves are lush and densely packed, supported by a high moisture level that has remained at 100% for much of the day. In the background, the oregano is also making steady progress, quietly filling in the gaps with its delicate foliage.

Light levels peaked at 1742.5 lux earlier today before mellowing to 652.5 lux this afternoon. Although reservoir data is currently unavailable, our individual soil sensors are providing the necessary insights to keep each herb on track. The greenhouse looks vibrant and productive as we head into the evening.
