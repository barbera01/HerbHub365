It has been a bright Sunday in the greenhouse. Temperatures climbed steadily from a morning low of 17.38°C to a peak of 25.42°C, while humidity dropped from 52.2% to a much drier 31.4% by late afternoon. 

The chilli plant is currently the star of the show, with two healthy green peppers now visible on its stems. Although its leaves appear a bit pale, the plant remains well-supported by its central stake. To its left, the basil continues to thrive, showing off a dense cluster of vibrant, bright green leaves.

Soil moisture levels shifted significantly today. While the basil remained consistently hydrated at 100%, the chilli and oregano saw their levels dip to 0% before being replenished. Recent readings show a healthy recovery, with the chilli at 82.19% and the oregano at 73.34%.

With light levels reaching 1615 lux and air pressure holding steady at 1022 hPa, it has been a productive day for growth. We will continue to keep a close eye on the moisture levels to ensure the chilli and oregano remain as vibrant as the basil.
