It was a bright and steady morning at Herb Hub 365 on June 30th. As the sun climbed between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM UTC, we saw the light levels jump significantly from a dim 0.8 lux to a much brighter 196.7 lux. This increased light brought the ambient air temperature up from a cool 20.17°C to a pleasant 22.87°C, while the humidity gradually dipped from 52.2% to 44.5%.

Our herbs are responding well to the changing conditions. The basil remains perfectly saturated at 100% soil moisture, while the chilli and oregano are showing very slight decreases as they begin their day. The chilli moisture dropped slightly from 78.51% to 77.53%, and the oregano moved from 60.41% down to 58.55% by the final reading of the morning.

Individual plant temperatures followed the warming trend of the air. The oregano and basil both reached highs just over 20°C by mid-morning, while the chilli stayed slightly cooler, peaking at 19.44°C. Interestingly, the water temperature in the system rose more than the air, starting at 18.69°C and ending the session at 22.25°C.

With the barometric pressure holding perfectly steady at 1018 hPa throughout the morning, the environment remains stable and conducive to growth.
