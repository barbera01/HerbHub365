On the morning of July 5th, the greenhouse enjoyed a steady start as temperatures rose from a cool 21.71°C to a comfortable 23.56°C by 8:30 AM. With humidity averaging 54.8% and atmospheric pressure holding at 1019 hPa, the environment remained calm and balanced, providing a perfect atmosphere for the herbs to wake up.

The chilli plant is a standout today, showing off a prominent, healthy green pepper hanging from its branches. Supported by a central wooden stake, it flourishes alongside the basil, which has become exceptionally lush. The basil’s dense, glossy foliage fills the left side of the frame, while the oregano’s smaller, rounded leaves are visible in the background, growing steadily.

Soil moisture levels remain excellent across the board. The basil is fully saturated at 100%, while the chilli and oregano are well-supported at 86.79% and 71.86%, respectively. A bright peak in light levels reached 298.3 lux around 7:30 AM, giving the plants a burst of morning energy before settling into a soft, productive glow for the rest of the day.
