As the sun climbed higher today, the greenhouse warmed steadily from a cool 19.04°C to a pleasant 23.29°C by midday. This shift was accompanied by a significant boost in light levels, peaking at 1907.5 lux. These bright, warm conditions provided our herbs with plenty of energy for a productive day of growth.

Soil moisture readings saw a welcome jump just before noon, suggesting the plants are now well-hydrated for the warmest part of the day. The basil is currently at 24.03%, while the chilli and oregano are sitting at 54.46% and 59.27%, respectively. As the air warmed, relative humidity gradually settled at a comfortable 40.5%.

Our latest snapshot shows vibrant, lush basil filling the frame with deep green, glossy leaves. In the foreground, the chilli plants display a bit of character with minor pale spotting and slight yellowing at the leaf tips, though their overall vigor remains strong. A splash of bright yellow from a ripening pepper in the background adds a cheerful contrast to the sea of green.

With atmospheric pressure holding steady at 1012 hPa, the Herb Hub 365 greenhouse remains stable and healthy. The plants are tucked into moist soil and enjoying the light, making for a peaceful afternoon in the indoor garden. We will continue to monitor these conditions as we transition toward the evening.
