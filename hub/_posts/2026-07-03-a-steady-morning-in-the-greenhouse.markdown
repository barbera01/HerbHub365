The morning of July 3, 2026, brought remarkable stability to the Herb Hub 365 greenhouse. Between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM UTC, atmospheric pressure held firm at a consistent 1024 hPa. This steady start provided a balanced environment for the herbs as the sun began its climb, ensuring a calm and predictable transition into the day.

Temperatures followed a natural morning curve, rising from 21.06°C to a comfortable 22.16°C. During this window, light intensity steadily increased from 84.2 to 187.5 lux. Humidity levels remained stable, averaging 48.84%, which kept the air perfectly tempered and prevented the environment from becoming too dry as the greenhouse warmed.

The herbs remain exceptionally well-hydrated. Our basil maintained a constant 100% moisture level, while the chilli and oregano pots showed only negligible fluctuations, ending the period at 85.9% and 94.8%, respectively. These minor shifts are a natural sign of the plants beginning their daily metabolic processes as they interact with the growing morning light.

With soil moisture high across all varieties, the greenhouse system is clearly maintaining a healthy equilibrium. It was a quiet, productive morning where every sensor reading pointed toward a thriving garden.
