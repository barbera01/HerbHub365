It was a bright, warming day at Herb Hub 365 as greenhouse temperatures climbed from a morning low of 23.86°C to a mid-afternoon peak of 28.85°C. Light levels mirrored this rise, starting at a dim 3.3 lux and reaching 146.7 lux during the brightest hours. As the heat increased, the air dried out, with humidity drifting from a morning high of 51.7% down to 39.5%.

Despite the drier air, the herbs remained steady. The Chilli plants averaged a comfortable 24.46°C, while the Basil and Oregano stayed closely matched in temperature throughout the day. The most significant shift occurred in the soil moisture; the Oregano started the morning thirsty at 47.92% but ended the afternoon well-hydrated at 85.65% after a much-needed drink.

The Basil remains fully saturated at 100%, while the Chilli moisture levels held consistent between 74% and 79%. With a stable barometric pressure of 1009 hPa, we are heading into the evening with a healthy, well-watered greenhouse.
