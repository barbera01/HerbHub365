It has been a bright, active day at Herb Hub 365. Midday light levels peaked at 17,460 lux, pushing the greenhouse temperature to a warm 31.91°C. Despite the ambient heat, the plants have stayed remarkably cool, with individual temperatures hovering comfortably between 19°C and 24°C.

The basil is showing excellent vigor, its broad green leaves reaching upward with health. Nearby, a small, firm green pepper is developing steadily among the chilli plant's foliage. Both plants appear vibrant and show no signs of heat stress despite the intense afternoon sun.

Soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano has held steady at 100%, suggesting the hydration system is keeping pace with the summer demand. Humidity dipped from a morning high of 54.6% to 32% during the warmest part of the day, while atmospheric pressure remained stable at 1019 hPa. We will continue to monitor the environment as the Hub begins to cool for the evening.
