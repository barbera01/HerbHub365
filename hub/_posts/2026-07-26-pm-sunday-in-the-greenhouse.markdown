It has been a productive Sunday at Herb Hub 365, with temperatures climbing steadily from a mild 21.4°C to a peak of 25.7°C by midday. This warming trend coincided with a surge in light levels, reaching 2255 lux and providing the herbs with a healthy dose of energy.

The basil continues to show impressive vigour, its bright green, well-hydrated leaves filling the left side of the frame. To the right, the chilli plant is making excellent progress; a small, firm green fruit is now clearly visible, supported by a wooden stake to help manage its growth.

As the light intensified, humidity naturally dipped from 60.9% to a comfortable 47.7%. This shift helps keep air circulating around the foliage, preventing the environment from becoming stagnant. 

Despite the warmth, soil moisture across the basil, chilli, and oregano remains perfectly stable at 100%. The root systems are clearly getting exactly what they need, evidenced by the lush condition of the leaves as the afternoon sun begins to mellow.
