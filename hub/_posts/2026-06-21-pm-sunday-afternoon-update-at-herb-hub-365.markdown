It has been a bright, warm day in the greenhouse. Since our early morning readings, the ambient temperature climbed from a cool 19.23°C to a peak of 26.96°C. Humidity levels remained consistent, hovering comfortably between 47.8% and 54.8% to provide a steady, reliable atmosphere for our residents.

The herbs soaked up plenty of sunshine today, with light levels reaching a maximum of 4127.5 lux. This midday brightness warmed the reservoir to a high of 31.13°C, yet the plants themselves stayed relatively cool. The oregano and basil peaked at approximately 24°C, while the chilli remained just below that mark throughout the heat of the day.

Soil moisture levels show some interesting variation across the beds. While the basil remains fully saturated at 100% and the chilli holds steady at 81.07%, we are keeping a close eye on the oregano. Its moisture dipped from a morning high of 77.65% to 58.58% by the latest snapshot, suggesting it is working hard in the afternoon sun.

Overall, the greenhouse is performing beautifully with stable atmospheric pressure holding at 1023 hPa. The plants appear to be thriving in these conditions, and we will continue to monitor the oregano through the evening to ensure its moisture levels stay within the ideal range.
