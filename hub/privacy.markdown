## Summary

Herb Hub 365 uses privacy-conscious analytics to understand which content is useful and how the site can be improved. Analytics remains optional, and this page explains what is collected, when it is collected, and how to control it.

<section class="privacy-grid" aria-label="Privacy details by topic">
  <article class="privacy-card" aria-labelledby="privacy-provider-title">
    <h2 id="privacy-provider-title">Provider and purpose</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Analytics service: <strong>Google Analytics 4 (GA4)</strong></li>
      <li>Measurement ID: <strong>G-CYF38MD43R</strong></li>
      <li>Purpose: measure page performance, understand aggregate visitor behavior, and improve editorial and technical quality</li>
    </ul>
  </article>

  <article class="privacy-card" aria-labelledby="privacy-consent-title">
    <h2 id="privacy-consent-title">Consent and legal basis</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Analytics is <strong>off by default</strong>.</li>
      <li>GA4 loads only after you explicitly select <strong>Accept analytics</strong>.</li>
      <li>If you select <strong>Reject analytics</strong>, analytics scripts are not loaded.</li>
      <li>Legal basis for analytics on this site is <strong>consent</strong>.</li>
    </ul>
  </article>

  <article class="privacy-card" aria-labelledby="privacy-data-title">
    <h2 id="privacy-data-title">Collected data categories (after consent)</h2>
    <p>When enabled, GA4 may process categories such as:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>page URLs and titles</li>
      <li>approximate location derived from IP (handled by Google)</li>
      <li>browser/device and technical metadata</li>
      <li>referral and engagement signals (for example, page interactions)</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Herb Hub 365 does not use this analytics data to sell personal information.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="privacy-card" aria-labelledby="privacy-storage-title">
    <h2 id="privacy-storage-title">Storage and cookies</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Your analytics preference is stored in <strong>localStorage</strong> as a first-party setting (<code>granted</code> or <code>denied</code>).</li>
      <li>GA cookies are only expected after consent, typically including:</li>
    </ul>
    <ul>
      <li><code>_ga</code></li>
      <li><code>_ga_&lt;container&gt;</code></li>
    </ul>
    <p>If consent is withdrawn, the site makes a best effort to disable GA and remove those first-party GA cookies for supported <code>herbhub365.com</code> domain variants.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="privacy-card" aria-labelledby="privacy-withdrawal-title">
    <h2 id="privacy-withdrawal-title">Withdraw or change consent</h2>
    <p>You can change your analytics choice at any time, without reloading the page.</p>
    <p>
      <button class="privacy-settings-button" type="button" data-analytics-open-settings aria-label="Open analytics cookie settings">
        Open Cookie settings
      </button>
    </p>
    <p>You can also use the persistent <strong>Cookie settings</strong> control in the site footer.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="privacy-card" aria-labelledby="privacy-retention-title">
    <h2 id="privacy-retention-title">Retention and contact</h2>
    <p>Analytics retention periods are configured within GA4 property settings and may change as configuration is updated. Herb Hub 365 aims to keep analytics settings proportionate to site improvement needs.</p>
    <p>For privacy questions about this project, contact: <strong>gardner@herbhub365.com</strong></p>
    <p>This page provides project-level transparency information and is not legal advice.</p>
  </article>
</section>
