The morning of August 11, 2026, brought a warm, steady start to the Herb Hub 365 greenhouse. Between 06:00 and 09:00 UTC, temperatures climbed from 20.8°C to a comfortable peak of nearly 23°C. Light levels rose significantly during this window, reaching 773.3 lux, while the barometric pressure remained perfectly stable at 1021 hPa.

The greenhouse is looking exceptionally lush. Our basil is a standout, displaying deep green, shiny leaves that signal strong vitality and growth. Nearby, the chilli plant is making wonderful progress; a bright orange pepper is ripening in the center, while a smaller green pepper develops further up the stem near its support. The foliage remains healthy and vibrant against the soft morning light.

Soil moisture for the basil held steady at 73%, though we noted some brief anomalies in our other sensors. Both the chilli and oregano reported sudden drops to 0% moisture between 08:00 and 09:00. Given the healthy appearance of the plants in our latest snapshots, these are likely temporary sensor fluctuations rather than a reflection of actual soil conditions.

Humidity levels averaged a comfortable 46.3% throughout the morning, providing a steady environment for the herbs to mature. While we will keep an eye on the moisture sensors to see if those intermittent warnings persist, the thick basil canopy and ripening chillies suggest a very successful day in the greenhouse.
