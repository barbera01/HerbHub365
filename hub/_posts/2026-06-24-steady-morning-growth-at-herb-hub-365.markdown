The morning of June 24, 2026, brought calm consistency to the greenhouse. Air temperatures averaged a comfortable 25.41°C, peaking briefly at 25.58°C. Humidity transitioned from a lush 59.9% in the early hours to a crisp 45.9% by mid-morning, providing the foliage with a refreshing cycle of hydration and evaporation.

Light levels remained modest but steady, reaching 80.8 lux during the brightest part of the morning. Atmospheric pressure held firm at 1015 hPa, offering a stable environment that allowed the herbs to maintain consistent internal temperatures. Under these conditions, the chilli plants remained the coolest in the collection, averaging 23.84°C.

Soil moisture data highlighted the diverse needs of our crops. The basil remained fully saturated at 100%, while the chilli plant showed the most activity, drying down from 91.2% to 76.98% as the day progressed. The oregano continued to thrive in its preferred drier soil, ending the session at 44.89% moisture.

System water temperatures were well-regulated at an average of 25.01°C, ensuring the roots remained protected from thermal shock. While specific reservoir volumes weren't recorded today, the overall vitality of the plants suggests the system is flowing perfectly. It was another successful day of growth at [Herb Hub 365](https://www.herbhub365.com), and we look forward to seeing how the herbs respond to tomorrow's conditions.
