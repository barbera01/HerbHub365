On the morning of September 8, 2026, the greenhouse experienced a steady climb in natural light. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, readings jumped from a dim 45 lux to a bright 910 lux as the sun rose. Throughout this transition, the environment remained comfortable for the herbs, with air temperatures averaging 21°C and humidity levels hovering between 49% and 54%.

The growing medium is currently fully saturated, with soil moisture sensors for the basil, chilli, and oregano all holding at a maximum of 100%. While specific reservoir data wasn't recorded today, the consistent saturation confirms the plants have ample hydration for the day ahead. This steady moisture level ensures the root systems are well-supported during the morning warming trend.

Visual progress in the greenhouse remains striking, highlighted by a bright orange chilli ripening in the background. While the foreground basil maintains a deep, healthy green, some larger leaves on adjacent plants are showing a paler, yellowish hue with slight browning at the tips. We will continue to monitor these color shifts to see if the pale coloring persists or requires a shift in care.
