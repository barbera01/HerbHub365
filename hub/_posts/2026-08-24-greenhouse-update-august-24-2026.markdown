The morning at Herb Hub 365 began with a cool, damp atmosphere as temperatures dipped just below 10°C and humidity peaked at 76%. By 9:00 AM, the greenhouse warmed significantly to a pleasant 20.61°C. This rise coincided with a steady increase in light, which climbed from a dim 42.5 lux to a much brighter 210.8 lux as the sun cleared the horizon.

The plants are looking exceptionally healthy today. The basil is showing off lush, vibrant green leaves, while the chilli plant provides a lovely splash of color with one ripening orange pepper and a smaller green one maturing higher on the stem. While the foliage is generally vigorous, we noticed some slight paleness on the upper leaves, likely a response to the changing light conditions throughout the morning.

Our soil sensors tell a story of two different zones. The chilli plant is perfectly saturated, maintaining a consistent 100% moisture level. However, the basil and oregano are currently returning 0% moisture readings. Given the healthy appearance of the basil in today’s image, this likely indicates a need for sensor calibration, though we are prepared for a watering session to keep up with rising daytime temperatures.

Overall, the greenhouse remains stable with atmospheric pressure holding at 1020 hPa. The plants are clearly enjoying the transition into the warmer part of the day, and we will continue to monitor moisture levels to ensure the basil and oregano stay as vibrant as they look in today’s snapshot.
