It was a bright, warm day at Herb Hub 365. On August 4th, the greenhouse air temperature peaked at 33.08°C, but the soil remained a cool sanctuary. Temperatures for the basil, chilli, and oregano stayed consistent, averaging between 22°C and 23°C despite the afternoon heat.

The visual progress in the greenhouse is encouraging. Our chilli plant is thriving, showing off a glossy green fruit near its support stake. Nearby, the basil is sporting thick, lush clusters of bright green leaves, appearing vibrant and full of life in these peak summer conditions.

Hydration levels remain perfectly balanced. Soil moisture for all three herbs held steady at 100%, even as the atmosphere shifted. We observed humidity dip from a morning high of 57.4% to 39.1% by the evening, following the natural warming cycle of the day.

As the sun sets, light levels have tapered off from a midday peak of over 12,000 lux to a quiet 33.3 lux. Everything is settled and stable for the night, with the plants looking vigorous and well-tended. Follow our live updates and growth journey at [herbhub365.com](https://www.herbhub365.com).
