The morning of August 10, 2026, brought a steady start to the greenhouse. Between 06:00 and 08:30 UTC, light levels climbed from 210.8 to 334.2 lux, signaling a bright day ahead. The ambient temperature warmed gently to a peak of 25.07°C, while humidity remained comfortably dry at an average of 39.9%.

Our soil moisture sensors reveal a tale of two climates. The oregano and basil are thriving in well-saturated soil, maintaining healthy levels between 69% and 71%. However, the chilli plant is experiencing much drier conditions, with moisture dipping as low as 23.85% before settling near 30.2% as the morning progressed.

Visually, the garden is a picture of health. The basil in the foreground shows off vibrant, deep green leaves, while the chilli plant features a prominent, bright orange pepper alongside a smaller green one developing further up the stem. Tucked in the background, the oregano continues to fill out with its signature small, rounded foliage.

With atmospheric pressure holding steady at 1015 hPa, the environment remains stable. We are keeping a close eye on the chilli plant to see if its lower moisture levels require intervention as the sun intensifies throughout the day.
