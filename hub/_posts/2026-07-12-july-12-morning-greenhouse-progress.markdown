The morning of July 12 was productive in the greenhouse as our sensors captured a steady transition into the day. This warming trend was accompanied by a noticeable drop in humidity, which began at a moist 60.1% and settled at 43.2% by the final reading of the session.

The chilli plant is also making great strides; a single, firm green pepper is developing well, supported by a wooden stake. While the chilli leaves appear a slightly paler shade of green compared to the basil, the overall vigour of the plants remains high.

Hydration levels across the pots are very stable. The basil is currently sitting at a maximum soil moisture level of 100%, while both the chilli and oregano are maintaining healthy levels in the mid-80s. These consistent readings suggest the environment is keeping the soil well-saturated for the varied needs of each herb, with the oregano tucked neatly into the background.

Light levels climbed steadily throughout the morning, peaking at 135 lux as the sun rose. With atmospheric pressure holding firm around 1024 hPa, the environment remains calm and conducive to growth.
