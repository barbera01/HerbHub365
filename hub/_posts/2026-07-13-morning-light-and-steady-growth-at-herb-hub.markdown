It was a calm and productive morning here at Herb Hub 365 on July 13th. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, we watched the greenhouse transition from the quiet of dawn into a bright, active space. The temperature rose steadily from a cool 19.43°C to a pleasant 21.59°C, while humidity levels remained comfortable, averaging around 51%.

The most notable change in the environment was the rapid increase in light. Our sensors recorded a jump from just 3.3 lux in the early hours to a much brighter 1270.8 lux by mid-morning. This shift is reflected in our latest snapshot, which shows the basil on the left looking exceptionally lush and vibrant. The density of the basil leaves suggests a very healthy growth phase.

In the center of the frame, the chilli plant continues to develop well, supported by its wooden stake. A single, smooth green chilli is now clearly visible and hanging prominently. While the chilli's leaves are a somewhat paler green compared to the deep hues of the basil, the plant appears sturdy. To the right, the oregano is filling in nicely, maintaining its low, bushy profile.

Our soil moisture readings remain high and stable across the board. The basil is currently sitting at 100% moisture, while the chilli and oregano are holding steady at approximately 85% and 88% respectively. With no environmental warnings to report, the herbs are enjoying a very consistent and well-hydrated start to the week.
