On the morning of August 12, 2026, the greenhouse at Herb Hub 365 saw a steady climb in temperature. Starting at a cool 23.99°C at 6:00 AM, the ambient air warmed to 25.62°C by mid-morning. Humidity remained remarkably consistent, hovering around 42%, while the barometric pressure held firm at a steady 1020 hPa.

Soil moisture levels tell two different stories today. The basil and oregano are sitting comfortably in the mid-to-high 70s, showing only a slight, natural decrease as the plants began their day. However, the chilli plant is notably dry, with moisture readings averaging only 10.98% and even dipping to zero briefly, suggesting it is in need of a good soak compared to its neighbors.

Visually, the greenhouse is vibrant and full of life. Despite relatively low light levels peaking at just 26.7 lux this morning, the foliage appears lush and healthy. The basil leaves are broad and a deep, glossy green, providing a beautiful backdrop for a bright orange chilli pepper that is clearly visible and ripening well.

With the environment remaining stable and the plants showing strong growth, it has been a productive start to the day. we will continue to monitor the chilli's soil moisture closely to ensure it stays healthy alongside the thriving basil and oregano.
