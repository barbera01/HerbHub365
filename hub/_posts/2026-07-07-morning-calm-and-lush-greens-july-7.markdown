The Herb Hub 365 greenhouse remained temperate today, with temperatures hovering steadily between 21.4°C and 22.3°C. Morning humidity started high at 52.3% before drying out to a crisp 38.6% by the final reading. This stable climate continues to provide a comfortable environment for our indoor garden’s steady growth.

Our latest snapshot reveals exceptionally vibrant greenery throughout the containers. The basil in the foreground shows off its signature glossy, deep green leaves, while the chilli plant reaches upward with broader foliage. Most excitingly, a young green pepper is now clearly visible hanging from the chilli plant, a promising sign of the plant's continued productivity.

Despite soft, ambient light peaking at just 15 lux, the plants appear vigorous and well-established. Set against the warm wooden flooring of their indoor home, they seem to thrive even in these quiet, dim conditions. The atmosphere remains peaceful as the herbs settle further into their respective pots.

Soil moisture levels tell two different stories this afternoon. While the basil and chilli plants are sitting comfortably at near 100% moisture, the oregano is significantly drier, averaging around 37.4%. We are keeping a close eye on the oregano to ensure it stays healthy and hydrated as the surrounding humidity levels shift.
