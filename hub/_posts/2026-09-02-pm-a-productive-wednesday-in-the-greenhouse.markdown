It has been a steady, warm Wednesday here at Herb Hub 365. Throughout September 2nd, the greenhouse environment remained comfortable for our residents, with temperatures climbing from a morning low of 20.39°C to a peak of 24.94°C. Humidity stayed within a healthy range, averaging a consistent 55% across the day.

The latest snapshot highlights a vibrant orange chili pepper ripening in the background, well-supported by its wooden stake. The surrounding basil plants look lush and vigorous, filling the center of the frame with healthy, broad leaves that provide a deep green contrast to the ripening fruit.

Light levels reached a maximum of 855.8 lux today, offering plenty of energy for photosynthesis before softening to 120.8 lux by late afternoon. The herbs appear to be thriving under these conditions, showing no signs of stress in their posture or leaf structure as the day winds down.

Sensor data confirms that soil moisture for the basil, chili, and oregano remained at a consistent 100%. This steady hydration is visible in the dark soil where irrigation tubing snakes around the base of the plants. With bed temperatures averaging 21°C, the greenhouse remains a perfectly balanced environment for continued growth.
