It was a calm morning at Herb Hub 365. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, the greenhouse environment remained stable and comfortable, with ambient temperatures averaging 23.59°C and peaking briefly at 24.22°C. Humidity levels stayed within a healthy range, hovering between 41.1% and 46.4%.

As the sun rose, light levels climbed steadily from 396.7 lux to a peak of 554.2 lux by 8:00 AM. This increase in morning brightness was accompanied by a minor shift in atmospheric pressure, which dipped slightly from 1018 hPa to 1017 hPa, where it settled for the remainder of the observation period.

Soil moisture across our herb beds showed healthy variation as the plants began their daily growth cycles. While the basil remained fully hydrated at 100%, the oregano and chilli both experienced a slight, natural decline in moisture. The oregano moved from 61.33% to 59.03%, and the chilli dipped slightly to 78.26%.

The plants stayed consistently cool compared to the surrounding air, with the basil, chilli, and oregano all maintaining internal temperatures around 22°C. Our water supply followed suit, averaging 22.46°C, ensuring that the roots were not shocked by any significant temperature swings during these early hours.
