It was a bright, steady morning in the greenhouse on July 29, 2026. As the sun rose, light levels climbed from a soft 401.7 lux at 6:00 AM to a much brighter 958.3 lux by 8:30 AM. The air temperature remained consistent, averaging a comfortable 24.38°C to provide a stable environment for the early growth spurt.

Our soil sensors for the basil, chilli, and oregano all reported 100% moisture, indicating the plants are currently fully saturated. The basil shows impressive vigor with bright green leaves reaching upward, while the chilli plant is making exciting progress. A small, firm green pepper is clearly visible and developing well near the top of the frame.

While the plants appear healthy, we observed a steady drop in humidity from 53.5% down to 37.7% by mid-morning. Some of the chilli leaves show a slightly pale or yellowish tint compared to the deep green of the basil, though the new fruit suggests the plant remains productive. Meanwhile, the oregano stays tucked away at the base, benefiting from a steady 22.8°C soil temperature.
