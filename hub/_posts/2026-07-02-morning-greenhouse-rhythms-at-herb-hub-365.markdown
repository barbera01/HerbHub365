July 2nd began with a steady climb in the greenhouse. As the sun rose, the temperature shifted from a crisp 19.89°C at dawn to a comfortable 23.4°C by mid-morning. Averaging a pleasant 21.72°C, the environment remained stable and inviting for the herbs' morning growth.

Light levels brightened significantly as the morning progressed, starting at a soft 55 lux and peaking at 172.5 lux around 8:30 AM. This increase in brightness coincided with a gentle dip in humidity, which moved from a morning high of 49.4% down to 43.6%. Meanwhile, atmospheric pressure held remarkably steady, hovering between 1013 and 1015 hPa.

The soil moisture readings indicate a well-hydrated start to the month. Our basil remains fully saturated at 100%, while the oregano and chilli are holding healthy levels at 95.27% and 86.17%, respectively. Though there were no new updates for the water reservoir today, the current soil conditions suggest our plants are thriving in their morning routine.
