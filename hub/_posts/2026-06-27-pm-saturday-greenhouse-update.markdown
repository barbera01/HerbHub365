It has been a warm, productive Saturday here at Herb Hub 365. On June 27, the greenhouse environment shifted significantly as the morning progressed. Air temperatures climbed from a cool 23.86°C at dawn to a peak of 28.34°C, while humidity dipped from 51.7% to a dry 38.3%. Light levels followed this upward trend, reaching a bright 19.2 lux before settling into the afternoon at a steady 16.7 lux.

Our herbs are thriving in the heat. The basil and oregano reached maximum temperatures of 26.13°C and 26.38°C, respectively, while the chilli plants maintained a comfortable average of 24.09°C. Even the system water warmed up, hitting a high of 27.19°C, ensuring the entire ecosystem remained active and engaged with the summer sun.

Soil moisture levels reflect the steady consumption of a busy afternoon. While the basil remains fully saturated at 100%, the chilli and oregano show the natural effects of transpiration. The chilli moisture dipped to 74.55%, and the oregano settled at 44.27%. These are healthy, expected ranges that indicate the plants are actively drinking as the greenhouse warms.
