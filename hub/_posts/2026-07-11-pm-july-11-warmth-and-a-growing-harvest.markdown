It has been a steady Saturday at Herb Hub 365. Sensors recorded a peak temperature of 27.87°C, providing a comfortable environment for the greenhouse residents. Humidity levels shifted naturally throughout the day, starting at a damp 61.2% in the morning and settling into a drier 52.9% by late afternoon.

Soil moisture remains consistent across the board. The basil continues to be the thirstiest plant, maintaining 100% saturation, while the chilli and oregano are well-hydrated at 84.4% and 89.1% respectively. With no system warnings and atmospheric pressure holding at 1021 hPa, the environment remains perfectly stable.

The visual highlight of the day is the maturing chilli plant. A bright green pepper is hanging prominently near the center of the frame, supported by a slender wooden stake to help manage its growth. To the left, the basil remains impressively lush, showing deep green leaves and great vigor.

Light levels reached their daily high of 97.5 lux around 5:00 PM UTC, catching the plants in a soft afternoon glow. The greenhouse is ending the day in a very healthy state, and we look forward to seeing how much more that chilli develops by tomorrow.
