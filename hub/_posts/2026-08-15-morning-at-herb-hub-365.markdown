The morning of August 15 brought a gentle rise in temperature within the greenhouse. Between 06:00 and 08:30 UTC, the air warmed from 20.4°C to a comfortable 21.5°C. With humidity steady at 51%, the environment provided a mild and stable start for the plants as they transitioned into the day.

The foliage appears exceptionally healthy in the latest snapshot. The basil is showing great vigor, its broad, glossy leaves catching the early light and filling the frame. Tucked among the leaves, the chilli plant is making its presence known with a bright orange pepper nearing maturity and a smaller green one just beginning to develop.

While the basil and oregano remain well-hydrated at 75% and 84% moisture respectively, the chilli plant is facing a dry spell. Sensors recorded moisture levels hitting 0% several times this morning, a critical dip that requires quick attention to ensure the developing peppers continue to flourish.

As the sun rose, light levels increased from 5.8 lux to 24.2 lux by mid-morning. The plants are generally responding well to the environment, and the greenhouse remains a vibrant space. We are keeping a close eye on the sensors to ensure the chilli soil returns to a healthy balance.
