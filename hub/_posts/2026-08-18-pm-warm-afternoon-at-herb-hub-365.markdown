The greenhouse enjoyed a steady transition into a bright afternoon today. As the sun climbed, light levels peaked at 3880 lux, bringing the ambient temperature to a comfortable 25.25°C. This warmth was accompanied by a refreshing drop in humidity, which settled at 47.3% by the afternoon.

Our plants are soaking in the rays and showing impressive vigor. The basil dominates the left side of the frame with its broad, vibrant leaves, while the chilli plant is the star of the day. A bright orange pepper is ripening beautifully in the center, with a smaller green one tucked away in the background.

Soil moisture levels reflect a busy day of growth as the plants drink steadily. Basil moisture dipped from 60.25% to 53.72%, and the oregano followed suit, moving down to 64.06%. Meanwhile, the chilli sensor remains at a saturated 100%, indicating it is either exceptionally well-watered or has reached its maximum reading.

Overall, the environment remains stable and productive. Herb temperatures have stayed slightly cooler than the surrounding air, hovering between 21°C and 22°C to ensure steady growth without heat stress. It is a peaceful afternoon in the greenhouse, and we are eager to see how that orange chilli progresses by tomorrow.
