The greenhouse saw a significant shift in conditions throughout the day on August 3, 2026. As the morning progressed into the afternoon, the air temperature climbed steadily from a cool 20.11°C to a peak of 31.29°C. This warming trend was accompanied by a sharp drop in humidity, which started at a moist 63% and dipped to 25.7% by the final reading of the session.

Light levels reached their highest point around midday, hitting 11,477.5 lux, providing plenty of energy for the plants during the brightest hours. Despite the heat and the dry air, the soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano remained perfectly stable at 100%. The plant sensors recorded internal temperatures between 18°C and 22°C, suggesting the herbs are managing the afternoon warmth quite well.

Looking at the latest snapshot, the basil in the foreground appears particularly vibrant, with its broad, bright green leaves showing excellent vigor. To the right, the chilli plant is also thriving; a small, firm green pepper is clearly visible nestled among the foliage. The overall growth across the hub looks healthy and lush as we head into the evening.
