The greenhouse enjoyed a steady start this morning. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM UTC, the air temperature averaged a comfortable 21.15°C, peaking briefly at 21.81°C. Our basil, chilli, and oregano plants trailed slightly behind the ambient air, maintaining internal temperatures between 19°C and 20°C.

As the morning progressed, light levels climbed steadily from a dim 82.5 lux to 350.8 lux. This gradual brightening was paired with stable humidity levels averaging 55.68%. These consistent environmental conditions provide a reliable foundation for the plants as they transition into their daily growth cycle.

Hydration remains excellent across the board. Soil moisture sensors for the basil, chilli, and oregano reported a consistent 100% saturation throughout the monitoring window. This indicates that the irrigation system is keeping the root zones perfectly hydrated and ready for the day's peak activity.

Visually, the herb bed is a portrait of vigor. Broad, lush basil leaves dominate the foreground with a vibrant green hue. Tucked behind a wooden support, a single chilli has ripened into a bright, cheerful orange, while the smaller, textured leaves of the oregano fill out the frame.
