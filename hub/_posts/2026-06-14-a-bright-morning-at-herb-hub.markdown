Sunday morning, June 14th, brought a steady glow to the greenhouse. As the sun climbed between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM, light levels rose from a dim 49.2 lux to a vibrant 224.2 lux. This natural radiance warmed the air comfortably, lifting the ambient temperature from a crisp 16.27°C to a pleasant 19.86°C by mid-morning.

The atmosphere shifted as the day began, with humidity levels easing from 56.4% to a fresh 45.6%. Throughout this transition, atmospheric pressure remained remarkably stable between 1016 and 1017 hPa. Below the surface, the water reservoir held a consistent 16.82°C, providing a reliable and steady source of hydration for the roots.

Our herbs showed varying needs as the morning progressed. The basil remained fully saturated at 100% soil moisture, while the chilli plants maintained a well-hydrated average of 82.58%. The oregano preferred drier conditions, with its moisture levels dipping slightly from 47.27% to 44.11% as the greenhouse environment settled.

Individual plant temperatures tracked the rising morning air while remaining just a few degrees cooler than the surrounding environment. Starting near 15°C, the basil, chilli, and oregano all warmed to over 17°C by 9:00 AM. It was a healthy, active start to the day here at [Herb Hub 365](https://www.herbhub365.com), with every plant finding its rhythm.
