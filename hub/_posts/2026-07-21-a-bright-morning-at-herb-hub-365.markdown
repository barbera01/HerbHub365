On the morning of July 21, 2026, the greenhouse enjoyed a vibrant transition into the day. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, light levels climbed from 663 to a peak of 2,459 lux. This surge in brightness was paired with a steady warming trend, as ambient temperatures rose from a cool 21.35°C to a comfortable 24.78°C by mid-morning.

Our herbs are responding beautifully to these conditions. The basil is showing excellent vigor with its broad, reaching leaves, while the oregano remains lush and dense. A particular highlight today is the chilli plant, where a small, healthy green pepper is now clearly visible nestled near its wooden support stake.

Hydration remains perfect, with soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano holding steady at 100%. As the morning warmed, air humidity dipped from 50% to 42.1%, creating a crisp and active environment for growth. With atmospheric pressure stable at 1025 hPa, it has been a remarkably steady and productive start to the day.
