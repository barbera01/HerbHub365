It has been a steady, warm afternoon at Herb Hub 365. The greenhouse remains comfortable, with temperatures peaking at 26.38°C and humidity averaging a balanced 49.6%. These stable conditions provide a gentle environment for the plants as we move through the first week of September.

Light levels reached 792.5 lux today, offering enough brightness to keep the herbs active without the stress of intense heat. Our sensors show soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano holding steady at 100%, ensuring the root systems stay fully hydrated during the warmest parts of the day.

The basil is a standout today, showing off lush, deep green foliage. The leaves appear vibrant and full of vigor, filling out the container with dense growth. It is clearly thriving in the current humidity and remains the most robust resident of the Hub this afternoon.

The chilli plant is also making progress, with a bright orange pepper ripening beautifully. While the fruit looks healthy, we are monitoring the surrounding foliage; some nearby leaves are a paler yellow-green compared to the basil, and there is a touch of browning on one leaf tip near the center.

Overall, the greenhouse remains a productive space. With consistent moisture and moderate temperatures, the herbs are well-positioned for continued growth. We will continue to monitor the light and temperature trends as the evening approaches.
