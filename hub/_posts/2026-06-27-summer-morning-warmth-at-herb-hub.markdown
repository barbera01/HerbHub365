On June 27, 2026, the greenhouse enjoyed a steady climb in temperature as the day began. Starting at 23.86°C at 6:00 AM, the air warmed to 26.43°C by 8:30 AM. Throughout this transition, the atmospheric pressure held steady at 1012 hPa, offering a stable foundation for the morning growth.

As light levels rose from a dim 3.3 lux to 10 lux, the greenhouse climate shifted naturally. Humidity levels gradually dipped from 51.7% to 47.8%, clearing the dampness of dawn. This drier, warmer air encourages the gentle circulation necessary for healthy herb development as the sun rises.

Soil moisture readings reflect the unique needs of each resident. While the basil remains fully saturated at 100%, the chilli and oregano began to transpire in the early light. The chilli moisture moved from 78.11% to 74.63%, while the oregano settled at 46.56%, showing active uptake as the plants woke up for the day.

Temperatures across the individual beds tracked closely with the ambient air. The oregano and basil reached highs of 24.5°C and 24.19°C, while the chilli stayed slightly cooler at 23.5°C. Even the system water warmed to 25.31°C, signaling a stable and productive start to the summer morning.
