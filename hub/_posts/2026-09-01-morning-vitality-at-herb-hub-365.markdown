The greenhouse hummed with activity on the morning of September 1. As the sun rose, light levels climbed from a dim 70 lux at 6:00 AM to a peak of 401.7 lux by 8:00 AM. This transition was accompanied by a steady warming of the air, with temperatures rising from a crisp 17.8°C to a pleasant 20.7°C.

Hydration levels remain excellent across the board. Soil moisture sensors for the basil, chilli, and oregano all reported a consistent 100% throughout the morning, ensuring the plants have the resources they need for the day ahead. Ambient humidity stayed within a healthy range, starting at 59.9% and gradually dipping to 50.6% as the greenhouse warmed.

Visually, the garden is looking incredibly lush. The basil is the star of the show, displaying broad, vibrant green leaves full of vigor. Tucked behind the foliage, the chilli plant is making great progress, with a bright orange pepper ripening well. Even the oregano in the lower corner is showing healthy, dense growth as it fills out its container.

It is a stable and productive start to the month. With consistent soil moisture and a steady climb in morning temperatures, the environment is perfectly dialed in for our herbs. We look forward to seeing how that orange chilli continues to develop in the coming days.
