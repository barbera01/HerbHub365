It has been a productive July 11 at Herb Hub 365. As the afternoon progressed, we saw a steady climb in temperature, rising from a morning low of 21.82°C to a midday peak of 27.61°C. This warmth was accompanied by a comfortable drop in humidity, which settled at 46.1% after starting the day much higher.

Despite the soft indoor light averaging just 17.17 lux, the herbs remain vigorous and healthy. The basil is particularly lush, its vibrant green leaves filling the frame. These lower light levels haven't dampened the plants' spirits, as they continue to thrive in their cozy indoor environment.

Our sensors show the herbs are exceptionally well-hydrated. The basil has maintained a consistent 100% moisture level throughout the day, while the oregano and chilli are holding steady at 90.04% and 84.56% respectively. These stable conditions ensure the plants can mature without any signs of stress.

A close look at the chilli plant reveals a bright green pepper developing nicely, supported by a wooden stake to help manage its growth. Everything in the greenhouse looks vibrant and well-tended as we head into the evening hours.
