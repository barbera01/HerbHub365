It has been a bright and productive day at Herb Hub 365. As the sun crossed the sky, greenhouse temperatures climbed from a cool 16.25°C morning low to a warm peak of 27.3°C. Light levels reached 4,036.7 lux during the midday height, providing ample energy for the herbs before settling to a quiet 158.3 lux this evening.

The air grew noticeably drier as the afternoon progressed. While the day began with 55.3% humidity, it averaged a crisper 41.74% through the later hours. We also noted a slight dip in atmospheric pressure, which moved from a steady 1014 hPa down to 1009 hPa by our final reading.

Our plants are responding well to these summer conditions, though soil moisture levels vary by variety. The basil remained fully saturated at 100% all day, while the oregano saw the most significant shift, dipping from 87.48% to 70.23% by early evening. The chilli plants stayed relatively steady, ending the day at approximately 81.93% moisture.

As evening settles in, the internal temperatures for the basil, chilli, and oregano are resting comfortably between 22°C and 23°C.
