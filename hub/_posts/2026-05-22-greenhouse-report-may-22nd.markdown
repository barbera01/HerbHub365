The greenhouse experienced a wonderfully stable day of monitoring. Environmental conditions remained quite steady throughout the sampling period, creating a predictable atmosphere for our herbs to flourish.

Ambient temperature ranged gently from 22.16°C to 22.81°C, while humidity held perfectly constant at 49.3%. Light levels also showed a nice progression, starting around 459 lux and peaking at 661.7 lux, indicating excellent natural light availability.

Our herbs settled into comfortable conditions. Basil averaged 19.23°C, chili averaged 19.19°C, and oregano averaged 19.55°C. The water reservoir temperature remained consistently warm at 20.64°C.

Soil moisture levels varied appropriately across the different plants. Basil maintained the highest average moisture content, near 89.62%, while chili stayed steady around 74.5%. Oregano showed the lowest, yet still consistent, moisture level at 64.67%.

Today’s readings confirm that all our herbs are receiving consistent care. The balanced parameters suggest the environment is perfectly set for continued healthy growth!
