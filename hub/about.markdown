<section class="about-section about-section--manifesto" aria-labelledby="about-manifesto-title">
  <h2 id="about-manifesto-title">A greenhouse for plants and ideas</h2>
  <p><strong>Herb Hub 365 is a personal learning and development project</strong> built around a small, sensor-equipped herb garden. It brings together horticulture, software engineering, cloud infrastructure, observability, automation, and AI in one practical environment.</p>
  <p>The goal is not to present a finished commercial product. This site is a living record of experiments: what worked, what failed, what changed, and what I learned while building it. The greenhouse gives every technical decision a real purpose—from collecting temperature and soil-moisture readings to processing images and publishing daily updates.</p>
</section>

<section class="about-section about-editorial-note" aria-labelledby="about-learning-title">
  <h2 id="about-learning-title">Learning in public</h2>
  <p>Herb Hub 365 is intentionally iterative. Parts of the platform may be rebuilt, replaced, or simplified as new techniques are learned. The journal and architecture notes capture that process openly rather than hiding the rough edges.</p>
  <p>This makes the site both a growing journal and a technical portfolio—a place to practise new skills, connect software to the physical world, and create something useful and enjoyable along the way.</p>
</section>
