It has been a warm and productive day at Herb Hub 365. Between 06:00 and 12:30 UTC, we observed a steady climb in ambient temperature, starting from a comfortable 24.84°C and reaching a peak of 28.43°C. Light levels followed a similar trend, beginning at a dim 38.3 lux in the early morning and brightening to 167.5 lux as the sun moved overhead.

As the air warmed, humidity levels saw a notable decrease, dropping from a moist 59.9% to 39.9% by midday. Despite these shifts, atmospheric pressure remained remarkably stable, holding steady at approximately 1015 hPa throughout the observation period.

Our plants are showing varied moisture needs today. The basil remains fully saturated at 100% soil moisture, while the chilli plants fluctuated between 76.3% and 88.21%. Meanwhile, the oregano is sitting in much drier soil, with its moisture levels drifting slightly from 46.75% down to 42.67%.

Individual plant temperatures have stayed slightly cooler than the surrounding air, which is a positive sign of healthy transpiration. The oregano reached 25°C and the basil hit 24.81°C at their warmest points, while our water supply peaked at 26.31°C. Everything at [Herb Hub 365](https://www.herbhub365.com) is looking stable as we head into the evening.
