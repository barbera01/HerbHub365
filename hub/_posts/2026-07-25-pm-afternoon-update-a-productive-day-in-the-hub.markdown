It has been a warm and consistent day here at Herb Hub 365. Between the early morning and late afternoon on July 25th, the greenhouse environment maintained a comfortable average temperature of 24.7°C, peaking at a high of 28.1°C. These steady conditions have provided a stable backdrop for the herbs to thrive as we move through the middle of the summer.

Looking at the latest snapshot, the basil is looking exceptionally vibrant, with lush, healthy green leaves filling the left side of the frame. On the right, the chilli plant is showing off some exciting progress; you can clearly see a small, firm green chilli developing near the top, held up by a sturdy wooden support stake. The plants appear vigorous and well-tended in the afternoon light.

Our sensors indicate that the soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano has remained at a steady 100% throughout the day. This consistent hydration is reflected in the plants' upright posture and overall health. Humidity levels fluctuated between 33% and 53.6%, while light levels reached a maximum of 1913.3 lux, ensuring the herbs had plenty of energy for growth.

As the day winds down, the temperatures are beginning to settle into a cooler range. The individual plant sensors for the basil, chilli, and oregano are currently recording stable temperatures between 22.5°C and 22.9°C. It has been a successful day of growth, and we look forward to seeing how that young chilli develops overnight.
