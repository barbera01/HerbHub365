On the morning of August 17, 2026, the Herb Hub 365 greenhouse transitioned beautifully into a bright, warm day. Between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM UTC, temperatures rose steadily from 21.5°C to a comfortable 23.6°C. Light levels followed this upward trend, climbing from a dim 98 lux to nearly 500 lux, providing the herbs with a perfect burst of energy to start their day.

The visual progress in the greenhouse is wonderful to observe. Our basil remains exceptionally lush, sporting vibrant green leaves with no signs of wilting. In the center of the frame, a chilli pepper has reached a saturated orange, signaling it is nearly ready for harvest, while a second green chilli continues to develop further back in the foliage.

Soil moisture levels remained stable across the board, ensuring the plants stayed well-hydrated during the morning warm-up. Basil and oregano averaged 68% and 79% moisture, respectively. Although we noticed a brief sensor glitch for the chilli plant at 8:30 AM, the reading immediately returned to its saturated 100% state by 9:00 AM, confirming that soil conditions remain optimal.

Overall, the greenhouse continues to be a healthy and vigorous environment. Average humidity stayed around 53%, which helped maintain the excellent leaf condition seen in our morning snapshots.
