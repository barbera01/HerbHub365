Since tracking began at 6:00 AM on August 29th, the greenhouse has enjoyed a productive morning. Temperatures rose steadily from a cool 22.09°C to a pleasant 25.13°C by midday, while light levels peaked at 3684.2 lux. This consistent increase in natural energy has fueled a strong morning growth cycle for our resident herbs.

Our sensors indicate that the basil, chilli, and oregano remain perfectly hydrated, with soil moisture levels holding firm at 100%. Humidity has averaged around 54%, maintaining a stable environment even as the air warmed. Interestingly, the internal plant temperatures for the oregano and basil stayed slightly cooler than the ambient air, averaging between 21°C and 22°C.

The latest snapshot reveals a sea of vibrant green. The basil leaves are broad and healthy, showing off a lush texture and deep color. Most notably, ripening peppers are now visible among the foliage, displaying a bright orange-yellow hue. The plants are vigorous and well-established, with dense growth nearly obscuring the support frames and sensor wiring.

As we head into the afternoon, atmospheric pressure remains steady at approximately 1000 hPa. A slight dip in humidity suggests the midday warmth is keeping the air crisp and clear. We will continue to monitor these conditions to ensure the herbs maintain their excellent momentum throughout the day.
