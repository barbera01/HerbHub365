The greenhouse enjoyed a productive start on August 25, 2026, as the morning sun steadily brightened the space. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, light levels climbed from a dim 59.2 lux to nearly 300 lux, while the ambient temperature rose to a comfortable 22.17°C. Humidity remained stable, settling at a healthy 52.2% by mid-morning.

A watering event just after 7:00 AM provided a vital boost to the soil. White irrigation tubing and sensor wiring are nestled among the stems, ensuring the system is ready for the day.

Plant temperatures stayed slightly cooler than the air, with the herbs averaging between 18°C and 19°C.
