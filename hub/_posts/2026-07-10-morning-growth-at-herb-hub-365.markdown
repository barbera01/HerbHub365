It was a calm, steady morning in the greenhouse on July 10th. The latest snapshot shows exceptionally vibrant and lush greenery, with the basil displaying deep green, glossy leaves that signal excellent health. In the center, the chilli plant proudly displays a maturing green pepper hanging amongst its broad leaves, looking vigorous and well-hydrated even in the soft morning light.

Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, our sensors captured a clear environmental transition. Humidity started at a high of 61.9% before settling into a drier 41.7% by mid-morning. During this same period, the temperature climbed gently from a cool 22.48°C to a warmer 24.36°C, providing a comfortable and steady environment for the herbs to thrive.

Soil moisture levels remain high across the board, ensuring the roots have constant access to water. The basil is currently at maximum saturation of 100%, while the oregano and chilli are holding steady at 96.6% and 88.3% respectively. Light levels remained low during these early hours, peaking at just 20.8 lux, which accounts for the moody, atmospheric lighting in the morning gallery.

Overall, the greenhouse is maintaining a stable and productive atmosphere. With consistent air pressure and high soil moisture, the plants are in a great position for continued growth. We will continue to monitor humidity levels as the day progresses to ensure the environment remains optimal for our basil, chilli, and oregano.
