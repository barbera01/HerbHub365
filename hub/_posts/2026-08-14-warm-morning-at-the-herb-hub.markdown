The greenhouse experienced a steady climb in temperature throughout the morning of August 14, 2026. Starting at a comfortable 22.88°C at 6:00 AM, the environment warmed to a peak of 25.89°C by 8:00 AM. As the air warmed, humidity shifted from a moist 60.9% to a drier 40.6% by the end of the observation period.

Visually, the garden is vibrant and productive. The basil plants are showing off lush, deep green leaves with excellent vigor. Most excitingly, the chili plant has reached a new milestone; while several green peppers are still maturing, a bright orange chili is now clearly visible, signaling that harvest time is fast approaching.

Soil health for the basil and oregano remains excellent, with moisture levels averaging 72.3% and 75.1% respectively. We are currently tracking a discrepancy with the chili soil sensor, which is reporting 0% moisture despite the plant appearing healthy and upright. A sensor maintenance check will be performed soon to ensure our data remains as reliable as our growth.

Light levels remained relatively low during these early hours, averaging around 31.38 lux, while atmospheric pressure held steady at 1013 hPa. It has been a calm, productive start to the day at [Herb Hub 365](https://www.herbhub365.com), with ripening peppers providing a beautiful splash of color against the greenery.
