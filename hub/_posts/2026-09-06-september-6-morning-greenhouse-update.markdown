Early morning at Herb Hub 365 brought a steady window of growth between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM. The environment remained remarkably stable, with atmospheric pressure holding firm at 1017 hPa and humidity levels averaging a comfortable 50.5%. 

As the sun rose, light levels peaked at 766.7 lux around 7:30 AM before softening toward the end of the session. Ambient temperatures followed a gentle climb, starting at 20.69°C and reaching a maximum of 21.96°C. The basil, chilli, and oregano stayed slightly cooler than the surrounding air, maintaining a steady range between 19°C and 20°C.

Hydration remains excellent across the board. Soil moisture for all three herbs held at a consistent 100% throughout the morning. While specific reservoir data was unavailable today, the fully saturated soil indicates that the plants are currently well-supplied with moisture.

Visually, the greenhouse looks productive and vibrant. The basil displays lush green leaves, while a bright orange chilli pepper has fully ripened in the background, providing a sharp contrast to the foliage. We did notice some slight paleness and a minor brown tip on the larger chilli leaves, which we will continue to monitor in the coming days.
