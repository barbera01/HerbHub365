Morning at Herb Hub 365 brought a steady climb in activity between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM UTC. As the sun rose, light levels surged from a dim 118.3 lux to a bright 2800.8 lux. This increase was accompanied by a gentle rise in ambient temperature, peaking at 22.95°C to provide a warm, productive start for the greenhouse.

Our leafy residents are showing plenty of progress in the morning light. The basil continues to flourish with broad, vibrant green leaves, though a few show a paler, yellowish-green hue. Tucked behind the foliage, the chilli plant is maturing well, with several bright orange peppers hanging prominently as they reach their ripening stage.

While humidity remained stable at an average of 60.5%, soil moisture levels vary significantly across the pots. The chilli plant is currently fully saturated at 100% moisture, while the basil remains at a comfortable 36%. These differences highlight the unique hydration needs of each plant within our shared environment.

We are keeping a close watch on the oregano, which has reached a critical low. After starting the morning at a modest 6.27%, its soil moisture dropped sharply to just 1.44% by 9:00 AM. This thirsty herb is in immediate need of watering to keep pace with its well-hydrated neighbors as we head into the afternoon.
