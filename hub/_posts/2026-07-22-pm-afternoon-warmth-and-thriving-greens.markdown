July 22 brought steady growth to Herb Hub 365. The greenhouse transitioned from a humid morning into a bright, warm afternoon, with air temperatures climbing from 19.86°C to a peak of 28.66°C. This shift provided a significant energy boost for the plants during the sunniest hours of the day.

Light levels peaked at 11,342.5 lux, while humidity dropped from a damp 56% to a drier 38.4% by late afternoon. Despite the rising ambient heat, internal plant temperatures for the basil, chilli, and oregano remained stable and cool, averaging between 20°C and 21°C.

Hydration remains excellent, with soil moisture levels for all three herbs holding steady at 100%. This consistent saturation ensures the plants are well-equipped to handle the afternoon warmth without stress, keeping the system balanced even as conditions shift.

Visually, the hub is flourishing. A developing green chilli is now clearly visible, hanging prominently from its stem. The basil shows great vigor with large, vibrant leaves reaching upward, while the oregano is beginning to spread nicely in the background. Everything looks lush and healthy as we move into the evening.
