It has been a productive day at Herb Hub 365 as the greenhouse shifted from a cool 21.44°C morning to a bright 28.3°C afternoon. As the sun climbed, humidity naturally dipped from 62.3% to a comfortable 44.6%, creating a crisp, stable environment for growth.

The light levels captured the day's energy, surging from a soft 114.2 lux to a brilliant 2287.5 lux at the midday peak. Our basil, chili, and oregano plants thrived in the warmth, with their individual temperatures rising into the mid-20s. Notably, the water temperature saw the most dramatic increase, climbing from 19.88°C to a high of 31.38°C.

Soil moisture management remained a priority throughout the day. While the basil stayed perfectly saturated at 100%, the oregano bed saw a significant boost, rising from a dry 41.95% to a healthy 86.09%. The chili bed followed suit, moving from 80% to full saturation, ensuring every plant is well-hydrated for the evening ahead.

With all systems stable and the plants soaking up the final rays of the afternoon sun, the greenhouse is in peak condition. We will continue monitoring these trends to maintain the ideal growing environment. Stay tuned to [herbhub365.com](https://www.herbhub365.com) for our next update.
