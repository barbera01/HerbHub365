The morning at Herb Hub 365 began with a cool, damp atmosphere. Between 6:00 AM and 7:00 AM UTC, greenhouse temperatures averaged a crisp 17.56°C. Humidity levels were notably high, peaking at 77.1%, providing a very moist environment for the herb collection as the day started.

Light levels remained low during the early hours, averaging just 55.3 lux. Despite the dim morning light, soil moisture sensors for the basil, chili, and oregano all reported a saturated 100%. The plants are fully hydrated, likely benefiting from both the high ambient humidity and recent watering.

Visually, the greenhouse is looking productive. The most striking feature in today’s snapshot is a vibrant, bright orange pepper showing excellent maturity. While the surrounding chili leaves appear a slightly lighter green, the basil in the foreground continues to show lush, dense growth.

Foliage checks reveal some slight yellowing and minor browning at the tips of a few leaves, which is common in high-humidity environments. However, the overall health of the herbs remains strong.
