The greenhouse enjoyed a steady start this July 31st. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM, temperatures averaged a comfortable 21.9°C with humidity levels hovering between 40% and 45%. This crisp environment provided a perfect start for the herbs as they began their daily growth cycle.

Light levels surged mid-morning, peaking at 2057.5 lux before settling back down. Despite these fluctuations in brightness, the soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano remained fully saturated. Interestingly, the plant roots stayed a few degrees cooler than the ambient air, averaging a steady 17°C throughout the observation period.

Our basil is showing impressive vigor, with lush, bright green leaves that are densely packed and thriving. Nearby, the chilli plant is making progress along its wooden support stake. It has successfully produced at least one firm green fruit, which is now clearly visible nestled among the foliage.

While the chilli leaves show a slightly lighter, more yellowish-green hue compared to the deep tones of the basil, overall growth remains robust. With stable air pressure and consistent moisture across all pots, the Herb Hub 365 system is maintaining an ideal balance for midsummer cultivation.
