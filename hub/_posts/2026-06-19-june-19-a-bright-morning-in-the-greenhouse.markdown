The morning of June 19 brought a steady start to the Herb Hub 365 greenhouse. Between 06:00 and 08:30 UTC, the environment remained remarkably stable, with ambient temperatures resting between 21.4°C and 22.2°C. Humidity levels were equally consistent, beginning at a morning high of 62.3% before settling to 59.6% as the day progressed.

Light levels followed a natural morning arc, starting at a soft 114.2 lux and climbing to 445.8 lux as the sun rose. This gradual increase in brightness was accompanied by steady barometric pressure, which held firm between 1006 and 1007 hPa throughout the observation window.

Our plants are showing distinct moisture profiles today. The basil remains fully saturated at 100% soil moisture, while the chilli plants are holding a healthy range between 80.6% and 83.5%. The oregano continues to thrive in drier conditions, maintaining a consistent moisture level near 44.5%.

Individual plant temperatures mirrored the ambient air but stayed slightly cooler than their surroundings. The chilli plants were the coolest at 19.69°C, while the oregano reached a leaf temperature of 21.44°C. With the system water averaging a stable 20.4°C and no warnings triggered, the herbs are well-positioned for a productive day ahead.
