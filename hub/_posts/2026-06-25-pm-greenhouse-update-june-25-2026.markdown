It has been a warm, steady day at Herb Hub 365. Greenhouse temperatures climbed from a morning start of 24.76°C to a peak of 26.73°C by the afternoon. As the air warmed, the atmosphere grew noticeably drier, with humidity levels dipping from 53.1% to a comfortable 41.8% by our latest check-in.

Despite soft light levels peaking at just 30 lux, the herbs have been quite active. While the basil remains fully saturated at 100% soil moisture, the chilli and oregano are drinking more greedily today. Soil moisture for the chilli dropped from a full 100% to 82.29%, while the oregano followed a similar trend, settling at 82.76%.

The plants are currently maintaining a cooler internal temperature than the surrounding air, resting comfortably between 23°C and 23.75°C. Meanwhile, the system's water temperature rose slightly to 24.94°C by midday, mirroring the general warming of the greenhouse environment.

We are monitoring the chilli and oregano closely as their soil dries to ensure they remain vibrant despite the lower humidity. Everything remains healthy and stable as we move into the evening. Follow our live updates at [herbhub365.com](https://www.herbhub365.com).
