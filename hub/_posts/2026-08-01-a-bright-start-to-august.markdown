It has been a productive morning at Herb Hub 365. As we moved through the early hours of August 1st, the greenhouse saw a significant increase in light levels, climbing from a soft 315 lux at dawn to a much brighter 1383 lux by 9:00 AM. This steady morning glow provided a wonderful boost to the plants as they began their day.

The environment inside has remained comfortable, with the ambient temperature warming from 19.8°C to 23.7°C. This rise was accompanied by a gradual decrease in humidity, which started at 48% and settled at 38%. Air pressure remained perfectly stable at 1015 hPa, providing a consistent atmosphere for steady growth.

Visually, the greenhouse is looking very healthy. The basil on the left is particularly striking, showing off lush, vibrant green leaves with great vigor. On the right, the chilli plant is making excellent progress; a small, firm green chilli is clearly visible hanging near the wooden support stake. 

Our sensors reflect this strength, with soil moisture for the basil, chilli, and oregano remaining at a consistent 100%. Plant-specific temperatures have also stayed within a healthy range, hovering between 18°C and 20°C. It is shaping up to be a fantastic day for our herbs as they continue to thrive in these stable conditions.
