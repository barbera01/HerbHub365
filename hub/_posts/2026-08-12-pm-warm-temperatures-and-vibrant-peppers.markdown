It has been a warm and active day here at Herb Hub 365. Our sensors recorded a steady climb in temperature, rising from a comfortable 23.99°C this morning to a peak of 28.15°C by midday. As the heat increased, humidity followed an inverse trend, dipping from a high of 42.4% to a much drier 33.7%.

The chilli plant is currently the standout of the greenhouse. A bright orange pepper is ripening beautifully among the deep green leaves, while a smaller green pepper is developing higher up the stem. Curiously, the soil moisture sensor for the chilli reported a sharp decline to 0%. Given the plant's healthy, upright posture, we are investigating whether this is a sensor calibration issue or a genuine need for immediate irrigation.

Meanwhile, the basil and oregano continue to thrive with consistent moisture levels. The basil remains lush and full at 70.27% hydration, while the oregano sits comfortably at 73.75%. Even with ambient light peaking at a relatively low 26.7 lux, the overall vigor of the greenery suggests the herbs are handling the current conditions well.

With atmospheric pressure holding steady at 1019 hPa, the environment remains stable as we head into the evening. We are keeping a close watch on the chilli's data to ensure the ripening fruit gets the support it needs. Check back tomorrow for more updates on our progress at herbhub365.com.
