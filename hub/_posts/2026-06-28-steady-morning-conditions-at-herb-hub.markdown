On the morning of June 28, 2026, the Herb Hub greenhouse experienced a calm and consistent start. Between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM UTC, air temperatures averaged a stable 24.48°C. As the sun rose, light levels climbed from a dim 4.2 lux to 75.8 lux, gently waking the plants for a productive day ahead.

Environmental conditions remained balanced as ambient humidity eased from 52.9% down to 47.5%. With barometric pressure holding steady at 1008.67 hPa and the water supply maintaining a consistent 24.23°C, the greenhouse provided a controlled, comfortable atmosphere for growth.

The herbs are showing excellent hydration levels across the board. Both the basil and chilli maintained soil moisture near 100%, while the oregano held steady at 87.38%. The plants themselves remained slightly cooler than the surrounding air, with internal temperatures ranging from 22.79°C to 23.3°C, indicating healthy transpiration as the light increased.

While water reservoir data was not recorded during this specific window, all other sensors confirm a thriving environment. It is a balanced start to the day at [Herb Hub 365](https://www.herbhub365.com), leaving our herbs perfectly positioned to handle the afternoon sun.
