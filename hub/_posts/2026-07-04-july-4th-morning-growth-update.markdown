It was a steady morning in the greenhouse today as our sensors captured a peaceful transition into the day. Between 6:00 AM and 9:00 AM UTC, the temperature climbed gently from 22.49°C to a peak of 24.13°C. Air pressure remained remarkably stable at 1020 hPa, providing a consistent environment for our developing plants.

As the sun rose, light levels increased significantly, starting at 102.5 lux and reaching 377.5 lux by the final reading. This brightness was accompanied by a slight decrease in humidity, which dipped from 51.8% to 49.7%. This balance of warmth and moderate humidity creates the ideal conditions for our herbs to breathe and grow.

Soil moisture readings indicate that the plants are well-tended and thriving. The basil remains perfectly hydrated at a consistent 100% moisture level, while the chilli and oregano are holding steady at healthy averages of 84.53% and 89.69%, respectively.

It is wonderful to see such stability across the greenhouse as we head into the afternoon. We continue to monitor these trends closely to ensure every pot stays as healthy as the basil. For live data and more updates on our progress, stay tuned to [Herb Hub 365](https://www.herbhub365.com).
